# Information Security Policies

## NovaTech Online Store

Version: 1.0

Owner: Information Security Department

Review Date: Annually

---

# 1. Password Policy

## Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to establish requirements for creating and maintaining secure passwords to protect organizational information assets.

## Scope

This policy applies to all employees, contractors, and third parties who access company systems.

## Policy Requirements

1. Passwords must contain a minimum of 12 characters.
2. Passwords must include uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and special characters.
3. Default passwords must be changed immediately upon first login.
4. Password sharing is strictly prohibited.
5. Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) shall be enabled for privileged accounts.
6. Passwords shall not be stored in plain text.

## Compliance

Failure to comply with this policy may result in disciplinary action and access revocation.

---

# 2. Access Control Policy

## Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to ensure that access to information systems is restricted to authorized users only.

## Scope

This policy applies to all company information systems and users.

## Policy Requirements

1. Access shall be granted based on business requirements.
2. The principle of least privilege shall be enforced.
3. Each user must have a unique account.
4. Privileged access must be approved by management.
5. User access rights shall be reviewed quarterly.
6. Terminated employees must have their access removed immediately.

## Compliance

Unauthorized access or misuse of privileges may result in disciplinary action.

---

# 3. Incident Response Policy

## Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to establish a structured approach for responding to cybersecurity incidents.

## Scope

This policy applies to all security incidents affecting company systems, applications, and data.

## Incident Response Process

1. Detection
2. Analysis
3. Containment
4. Eradication
5. Recovery
6. Lessons Learned

## Policy Requirements

1. All security incidents must be reported immediately.
2. Security incidents shall be classified according to severity.
3. Incident records shall be maintained for audit purposes.
4. Corrective actions shall be tracked until closure.

## Compliance

Failure to report security incidents may result in disciplinary action.
