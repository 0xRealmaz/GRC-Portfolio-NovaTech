# NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF) Assessment – NovaTech Online Store

## Overview
This assessment evaluates the cybersecurity maturity of NovaTech using the NIST CSF framework.

---

## Assessment Table

| Function | Current Status | Maturity Level | Gap | Recommendation |
|----------|----------------|----------------|-----|----------------|
| Identify | No asset inventory or formal risk tracking | Low | Lack of visibility over assets and risks | Implement asset inventory and risk management process |
| Protect | Weak access control and no MFA | Low | Insufficient protection of accounts and data | Implement RBAC and Multi-Factor Authentication |
| Detect | No logging or monitoring system | Low | No ability to detect security events | Deploy centralized logging and SIEM solution |
| Respond | No incident response plan | Low | No structured response to security incidents | Develop Incident Response Plan |
| Recover | No backup or disaster recovery process | Low | High risk of data loss and downtime | Implement backup and disaster recovery strategy |

---

## Summary

The organization demonstrates low cybersecurity maturity across all NIST CSF functions.

Key gaps include:
- Lack of asset visibility
- Weak access controls
- No monitoring capabilities
- No incident response process
- No backup and recovery strategy
